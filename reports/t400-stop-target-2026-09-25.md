# T-400: visible training stop target

**Date of review:** 25.09.2026. **Measured on base:** `65c5adfbc55066cf5bbfecd36d54728fec850d3b` plus this feature commit.

The amber **STOP** board hangs at each interior station's stop coordinate from
the existing package A axis. It is a neutral training cue, not a representation
of STIB/MIVB signage or a claim about the boarding side. It reuses the neutral
board generated by `tools/blender/station_board.py`; no `data/` file changed.
Its centre follows the active track's 2.10 m lateral offset from the route
axis while retaining the exact station chainage. A curved-axis test checks
the longitudinal, lateral and vertical projections of this placement.

## Renderer evidence

After `bash tools/dev/prepare-playable.sh build/t400`, a Debug game build and
Godot C# editor import, the following hidden Xvfb/OpenGL3 captures used Godot
4.7.2 at 1280×720:

```bash
xvfb-run -a "$GODOT_BIN" --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 1280x720 \
  --path src/Game -- --shot="$PWD/build/t400/PARC_near_cab.png" \
  --at-chainage=4060 --view=cab
xvfb-run -a "$GODOT_BIN" --rendering-driver opengl3 --resolution 1280x720 \
  --path src/Game -- --line --limit-kmh=70 \
  --shot="$PWD/build/t400/PARC_line_cab.png" \
  --at-chainage=4075.66 --view=cab
```

The [approach frame](visual-evidence/t400-stop-target/PARC_near_cab.png) was
inspected: at chainage 4060.1 m the amber STOP board is legible above the track,
16 m before Parc, and distinct from the black Parc/Park name boards. The
[line stop frame](visual-evidence/t400-stop-target/PARC_line_cab.png) was also
inspected: at chainage 4075.4 m, speed 0.0 km/h, after 54,449 ticks, the HUD
shows open doors and a −0.31 m stop error. The STOP board is directly overhead
and outside the cab's field of view; the HUD continues to show the stop state.
The [manifest](visual-evidence/t400-stop-target/manifest.json) pins both PNG
hashes and sizes.
Both frames were captured again after correcting the lateral placement. The
approach image changed; the final-stop image retained its previous SHA-256.

The generated plate was additionally rendered in Blender from iso, side,
normals and inside views. Iso showed a solid flat plate; side showed its thin
profile; the normals view was uniform on its face; inside showed the plate edge
because the inspection camera lay in its zero-thickness plane. These renders
are generated files under `build/`, so they are not committed. This screenshot
pair shows an approach and a final stop, not continuous manual driving.
